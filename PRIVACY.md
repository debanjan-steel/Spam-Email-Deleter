# Privacy Policy for Spam Email Deleter

**Effective Date:** 2026-11-08

This application ("Spam Email Deleter") is a personal mail hygiene utility created to help users detect and manage unsolicited bulk emails in their own Gmail accounts.

## Data Collection & Usage
- **Email Access**: The app accesses Gmail message headers and snippets exclusively to classify unwanted emails and allow users to move them to Trash.
- **Data Storage**: All email inspection and classification is performed locally in the user's browser session. No personal email content, contact lists, or messages are stored on third-party tracking servers or sold to advertisers.
- **Authentication**: Authentication is handled directly via Google's official OAuth 2.0 system. The app never asks for or stores your personal Google password.

## Third-Party Services
This application utilizes Google APIs in accordance with the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy).
