2. Progressive Web App (PWA) Install:

   - Click the "Install App" icon in the address bar (or Menu > "Install Gmail Spam Deleter")

3. Cloud Deployment (Render / Vercel / Railway):
   - To host in the cloud 24/7, use the included 'render.yaml' or 'Dockerfile'.
   - Once deployed, your cloud URL (e.g., https://your-spam-deleter.onrender.com) will be accessible from any phone or computer.
